THE STORY

Why a phone?

As an immigrant, quality time with family started to mean something different to me. Distance has a way of making you appreciate the little moments — the conversations, the stories and the voices you wish you could keep a little longer.

I wanted to create something that gives those moments meaning.

Some calls are worth keeping.
Better yet, on video.

And sometimes, talking to a phone feels easier than talking to a camera. Without a lens staring back at you, people can relax, open up and say the things they really mean.

Some calls are worth keeping.
Better yet, on video.
